How it works

  1. Bring your bike in any time we are open. No appointment needed.
  2. Kwame inspects it and calls or messages you with the problem and the price.
  3. You approve. Nothing happens without your go-ahead.
  4. We fix it, and let you know when it is ready to collect.

Bigger repairs that need the bike overnight have a storage fee of GH₵ 10 per day.

Common questions

How long will my repair take?

Most basic repairs and tune-ups are done the same day, usually within an hour or two. Bigger jobs like gear overhauls or full rebuilds may need the bike overnight or for a couple of days. We will give you an honest estimate when you drop it off.

Do I need to book in advance?

No. Walk-ins are welcome during opening hours. For a large or urgent job, call ahead so we can make sure we have the right parts in stock.

What types of bike do you work on?

All types: road, mountain, hybrid, BMX and kids' bikes, and most major brands. If you have something unusual, get in touch first and we will tell you if we can handle it.

About Kwame and Wheelhouse

Kwame opened Wheelhouse in 2019 after years of fixing bikes for friends and neighbours out of his yard. The workshop now handles everything from kids' bikes to high-end road and mountain bikes, and every repair is done by Kwame or under his direct supervision.

We also stock lights, helmets, locks, pumps and basic spare parts. If we do not have what you need, we can usually get it within a few days.
